## Recommended low-cost purchasing rule
Do not purchase a precision balance, chemical reagents, or dedicated corrosion sensors for this pilot. Prioritize:
1. Identical, uncoated steel coupons (or same-batch nails only if coupons are not affordable).
2. Reusable transparent containers.
3. A repeatable measuring tool for liquid volume.
4. A simple phone stand and stable diffuse LED light only if not already available.
5. A printed scale/color reference.

Get prices locally before buying. The project should remain within the user's stated €50 ceiling; if the suitable coupons or imaging setup exceed that ceiling, reduce the experiment scope rather than weakening measurement quality.

## Decision gate before spending
Proceed only if:
- specimen material and coating status can be described honestly;
- all specimens can be prepared and photographed consistently;
- exposure conditions can be repeated and documented;
- the image workflow can be applied consistently;
- safety and storage are manageable.

If these conditions cannot be met, pause the physical experiment and continue with a desk-based design study. Do not manufacture data.

## Suggested immediate sequence
1. Confirm the available smartphone and whether a stable lamp/stand are already available.
2. Compare the cost of 9 identical flat steel coupons versus 15 same-batch nails.
3. Choose either the 3-group feasibility study or the 5-group exploratory design before starting.
4. Take baseline photographs before any exposure.
5. Keep all raw data and images in the repository; never overwrite originals.
